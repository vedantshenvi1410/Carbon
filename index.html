    Traceable inputs. No accuracy promise.

    Carbonwise produces transparent estimates with source status, stated boundaries, and explicit engineering validation flags. This prototype is not certification or engineering sign-off.

    Calculation record

    Every displayed result is read from one calculation object. Full whole-life carbon is withheld until all required A–C modules have sourced values.

    Embodied carbon

    A1–A3 = Σ(material quantity × product factor). A4 and A5 use separate transport and site-energy inputs. B1–B5 and C1–C4 remain separate. Module D is outside the A–C total.

    Operational carbon · B6

    Each study year = annual electricity kWh × that year's kgCO₂/kWh. Annual consumption is held constant. Constant-grid uses the CEA historical FY 2025–26 weighted average; moderate/high are user-defined exponential annual declines, not forecasts.
